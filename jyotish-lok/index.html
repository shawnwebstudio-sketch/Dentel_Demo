FAQFrequently asked questions

What is a kundli?

A kundli (janam kundli) is a map of where the nine grahas were at the exact moment and place of your birth. It shows the 12 bhavas (houses) of your life, from career and wealth to marriage and health, and is the base of every reading we do.

How do I talk to an astrologer?

Tap any WhatsApp button on this page, or call us on 99280 89307. Share your birth details and question, and we'll fix a time for a call, chat or video session. You can also consult through the Jyotish Lok app.

What services do you offer?

Kundli analysis, kundli milan for marriage, daily and yearly rashifal, palmistry (hastrekha), vastu consultation, muhurat, online puja booking, and remedies such as gemstones, rudraksha and bracelets.

Can I book a puja online?

Yes. Choose a puja above and send it on WhatsApp with your name and gotra. Our pandits perform it on the shubh muhurat and send you photos and video of the puja.

Will my details stay private?

Yes. Your birth details and questions are used only for your reading and are never shared.
