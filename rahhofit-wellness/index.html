Meet your nutritionist

Hi, I'm Bijall. I help women eat well, not less.

Rahhofit Wellness was founded by Certified Nutritionist Bijall Kabra to help women 40+ reach sustainable weight loss through personalised Indian nutrition and holistic wellness.

Every plan is 1:1 and built around your kitchen, your family's meals and your body's changes. That includes perimenopause and menopause, when belly fat, cravings, poor sleep and low energy can show up even if nothing else has changed.
